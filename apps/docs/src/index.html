A proposed open protocol stewarded by paper&slate

One public doorway for an educational organization.

Educational Organization Manifest (EOM) lets schools, districts, colleges, universities, and training providers publish deliberately public institutional information through one predictable HTTPS resource.

Working draft
The educational-organization-manifest suffix is proposed and is not claimed as IANA-registered. Conformance does not prove truth, quality, accreditation, or legal compliance.

Why EOM exists

Predictable discovery

Consumers begin at /.well-known/educational-organization-manifest, then follow independently validatable resources.

Rich, linked modules

The root stays compact. Courses, offerings, programs, calendars, services, policies, news, meals, jobs, and more live in optional modules.

Public by design

EOM is not a student information format. Privacy boundaries exclude student records, grades, private schedules, credentials, and internal endpoints.

Protocol commitments

  • JSON Schema 2020-12 is the structural source of truth.
  • Canonical wire output is deterministic UTF-8 JSON; YAML is authoring convenience.
  • HTTPS origin is root authority; delegated resources are explicit, scoped, time-bounded, and non-transitive by default.
  • Provenance, effective dates, review states, and conflicts are first-class.
  • Signatures are optional integrity metadata, not a proof of factual truth.
EOM is neutral protocol infrastructure. Stewarded by paper&slate; independently implementable without a paper&slate account or runtime.
