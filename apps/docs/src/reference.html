Implement the protocol

The versioned specification and schemas are the implementation contract. This page is a map; use the immutable files in spec/1.0 and schemas/1.0 for exact details.

Behavioral rules

Identifiers

Use absolute stable URIs, retain external identifiers with their scheme, and do not confuse a delegated host with the educational organization.

Internationalization

Use BCP 47 language tags, localized values, direction metadata, international addresses, periods, timezones, and jurisdiction-aware levels and credits.

Validation

Structural validation comes from JSON Schema 2020-12. Semantic and policy findings are separate, pointer-addressed, and actionable.

Integrity

Detached JCS/Ed25519 signatures are optional. Verify canonical bytes, digest, key lifecycle, subject, authority, and expiry independently.

Module families

Organization profiles, campuses, departments, staff and role contacts, courses, offerings and sections, programs, calendars, events, facilities, services, policies, admissions, sports, transportation, meals, clubs, jobs, news, public statistics, and API/service discovery are all optional and independently omittable.
