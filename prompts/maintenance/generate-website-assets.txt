PROMPT ID: eom/generate-website-assets
PURPOSE: Generate public website/catalog assets from approved EOM data without creating a second source of truth.

INPUTS
- Approved EOM manifest/source: {{TARGET}}
- Output types: {{WEBSITE_JSON|STATIC_PAGES|PDF_INPUT|SCHEMA_ORG|EMBEDS|ALL}}
- Brand/site configuration: {{SITE_CONFIG}}
- Languages: {{LANGUAGES}}
- Destination review directory: {{OUTPUT}}

RULES
- EOM data is the structured source of truth for institutional facts.
- Site-specific presentation copy may be separate but must not silently alter factual fields.
- Generate deterministic assets.
- Preserve stable canonical links and IDs.
- Generate accessible text, headings, table structures, and image alternatives.
- Produce Schema.org Course/EducationalOrganization/CourseInstance mappings according to the mapping guide.
- Keep courses separate from offerings.
- Omit non-public/internal fields.
- Display freshness/effective dates where material.
- Display source/provenance links only according to site policy.
- Do not publish, deploy, or overwrite production assets automatically.
- Provide a diff and validation report.

OUTPUTS MAY INCLUDE
- route/data map;
- static course and department pages;
- catalog search index;
- printable-catalog intermediate data;
- JSON-LD;
- embeddable course cards;
- RSS/Atom/JSON Feed adapters for news/events where defined;
- sitemap entries;
- accessibility report;
- stale-data warnings;
- generated-file manifest.
