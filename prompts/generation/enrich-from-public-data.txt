PROMPT ID: eom/enrich-from-public-data
PURPOSE: Enrich a school EOM candidate with approved authoritative public data while preserving source authority and conflicts.

INPUTS
- Organization identity: {{ORGANIZATION}}
- Approved public datasets/APIs: {{DATASETS}}
- Target modules/fields: {{TARGETS}}
- Existing source tree: {{SOURCE_TREE}}
- Jurisdiction: {{JURISDICTION}}

REQUIREMENTS
- Use only datasets whose access and reuse conditions have been reviewed.
- Record dataset version, retrieval time, query, record ID, license, and transformation.
- Resolve entities using identifiers and corroborating attributes; never name-match alone.
- Government identity records may verify official identifiers, but do not automatically override organization-origin course or program descriptions.
- Preserve conflicting claims.
- Keep raw dataset fields separate from normalized EOM values.
- Publish only public aggregate statistics and apply suppression/small-cell rules.
- Do not enrich people using data brokers or unrelated public profiles.
- Do not infer demographic, disability, political, religious, or other sensitive attributes.
- Add field-level provenance for enriched values.
- Run validation, privacy, and conflict checks.
- Produce a review-ready candidate only.

OUTPUT
- dataset/source inventory;
- entity-resolution report;
- selected and rejected matches;
- enriched claims;
- conflicts;
- transforms/crosswalks used;
- privacy/statistical disclosure report;
- candidate diff;
- required reviewers.
