PROMPT ID: eom/privacy-review
PURPOSE: Perform a publication-focused privacy and safety review of EOM source and output.

TARGET
- Source/publication: {{TARGET}}
- Jurisdictional policy references supplied by operator: {{POLICIES_OR_NONE}}
- Modules: {{MODULES_OR_ALL}}

THIS IS A TECHNICAL/PUBLICATION REVIEW, NOT LEGAL ADVICE.

CHECK FOR
- student names, IDs, schedules, grades, attendance, discipline;
- IEP, 504, SEN, disability, medical, safeguarding, accommodation, or counseling data;
- private contact details;
- named staff fields without deliberate-public evidence;
- photographs/biographies without review metadata;
- individual transportation assignments or precise sensitive stops;
- facility access/security details;
- meal/medical data tied to individuals;
- small-cell aggregate statistics;
- internal systems, tokens, credentials, private URLs;
- hidden content in source files or generated artifacts;
- personal data in Git history, fixtures, logs, snapshots, or reports;
- unsafe rich text or remote embeds;
- unintended fields introduced through extensions.

A finding is not cleared merely because the source was public.

OUTPUT
- critical/high/medium/low findings;
- exact file and JSON Pointer;
- evidence;
- immediate containment action;
- proposed remediation;
- owner;
- whether history rewrite or secret rotation is required;
- clean-publication attestation only if supported by completed checks.

Do not expose sensitive values in the report. Redact them.
