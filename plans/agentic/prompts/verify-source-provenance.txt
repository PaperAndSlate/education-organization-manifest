PROMPT ID: eom/verify-source-provenance
PURPOSE: Verify that published or candidate EOM claims are supported by traceable sources.

TARGET
- EOM source/publication: {{TARGET}}
- Evidence ledger: {{LEDGER}}
- Allowed sources: {{SOURCE_SCOPE}}

TASK
For each material public field:
1. locate the corresponding claim/evidence record;
2. verify source identity and current reachability where permitted;
3. verify the evidence locator;
4. verify transformation and effective date;
5. verify authority class;
6. verify review decision;
7. identify stale or contradictory evidence;
8. identify fields with no provenance where the profile requires it.

Classify each field:
- verified;
- verified but stale;
- supported by lower-authority source;
- conflicting;
- transformed with documented mapping;
- inferred and not publication-ready;
- unsupported;
- unverifiable.

Do not expand source scope without operator approval. Do not copy full source documents.

OUTPUT
- provenance coverage percentage by module;
- unsupported-field list with JSON Pointers;
- stale evidence;
- source failures;
- conflict list;
- recommended remediation;
- no source or publication changes unless separately requested.
