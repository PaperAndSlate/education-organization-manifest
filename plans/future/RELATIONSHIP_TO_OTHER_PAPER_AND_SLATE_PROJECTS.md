# Relationship to Other paper&slate Projects

## EOM

Describes public educational organizations and discoverable resources.

Examples:

- school;
- district;
- department;
- course catalog;
- calendar;
- facilities;
- services.

## Future educational content/document format

Describes portable educational content:

- course plans;
- units;
- lessons;
- activities;
- assessments;
- rubrics;
- worksheets;
- presentations;
- resources.

A course in EOM may link to public content packages, but EOM does not embed an entire lesson-content standard.

## Future standards and concept API

May provide:

- curriculum frameworks;
- standards items;
- subjects/topics;
- competencies;
- crosswalks;
- qualifications.

EOM references stable identifiers from that service or other authoritative standards systems.

## Future school/index API

Aggregates observed EOM publications and government sources with provenance.

## Shared foundations

Projects should share:

- identifier conventions;
- localized values;
- provenance vocabulary;
- effective periods;
- licenses;
- code lists;
- conformance philosophy;
- governance where appropriate.

They should not be forced into one giant schema or release cycle.
