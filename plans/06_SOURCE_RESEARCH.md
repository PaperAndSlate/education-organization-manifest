## Research rules

- Prefer normative standards, official registries, and primary project documentation.
- Do not reproduce trademarked conformance marks.
- Document mappings rather than claiming equivalence.
- Track version and retrieval date for every external vocabulary or schema used.
- Treat current-version claims as time-sensitive.
