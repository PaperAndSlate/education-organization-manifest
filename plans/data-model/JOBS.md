# Jobs and Opportunities

## Job posting

Fields:

- id;
- title;
- hiring organization;
- department;
- location/campus;
- employment type;
- schedule classification;
- description or canonical posting URL;
- responsibilities summary;
- qualifications;
- compensation only when published;
- benefits summary;
- application URL;
- posted date;
- closing date;
- status;
- contact role;
- equal-opportunity statement link;
- accessibility/accommodation contact;
- language;
- provenance.

## Status

- draft;
- open;
- closing-soon;
- closed;
- filled;
- cancelled;
- evergreen.

## Privacy

Do not include:

- applicant records;
- hiring-manager private details;
- interview schedules;
- internal requisition data unless explicitly public.

## Freshness

Jobs expire quickly. A linter should warn when:

- closing date passed but status remains open;
- no closing or review date exists;
- application URL is broken;
- source has not been reviewed recently.

## Other opportunities

Future extension:

- volunteering;
- internships;
- vendor opportunities;
- tenders/procurement.

Keep v1 job posting focused.
