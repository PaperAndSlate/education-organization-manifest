# Facilities, Services, Policies, and Documents

## Facility

Examples:

- library;
- science laboratory;
- culinary kitchen;
- workshop;
- gymnasium;
- sports field;
- auditorium;
- art studio;
- music room;
- computer lab;
- counseling center;
- childcare facility.

Fields:

- id;
- name;
- type;
- campus;
- description;
- capabilities;
- accessibility;
- public hours;
- public booking information;
- images;
- capacity only when safe;
- status;
- public contact;
- effective period.

Do not publish detailed security layouts, access systems, or vulnerabilities.

## Capability

Facility capability may use namespaced terms:

- commercial kitchen;
- wheelchair access;
- hearing loop;
- changing facilities;
- maker equipment;
- specialized lab;
- public Wi-Fi.

## Service

Examples:

- counseling;
- library services;
- accessibility support;
- language support;
- career guidance;
- technology support;
- childcare;
- health office public information;
- food service;
- transportation support.

Fields:

- id;
- name;
- description;
- provider;
- locations;
- audience;
- eligibility policy;
- hours;
- contact;
- access/application link;
- cost;
- languages;
- accessibility;
- status.

Do not include service recipient records.

## Policy/document

Fields:

- id;
- title;
- category;
- organization scope;
- document URL;
- media type;
- language;
- version;
- effective date;
- review date;
- supersedes/supersededBy;
- summary;
- accessibility;
- license/publication terms;
- digest;
- provenance.

Example categories:

- handbook;
- admissions;
- attendance policy;
- acceptable use;
- accessibility;
- privacy;
- curriculum;
- fees;
- transportation;
- meals;
- athletics;
- safeguarding public policy;
- nondiscrimination.

## Document extraction

Agents may use linked documents as sources but should not copy entire copyrighted documents into EOM. Publish metadata, summaries where allowed, and provenance.
