# EOM 1.0 Privacy and Public Publication

EOM is public, redistributable, and likely to be cached. It MUST NOT contain student names/IDs, grades, individual attendance, discipline, IEP/504/SEN/disability/medical/safeguarding/accommodation records, individual schedules or transport assignments, applicant/waitlist records, private employee records, credentials, internal network details, or security-sensitive facility plans.

Named staff is opt-in and deliberate-public. Prefer role contacts. When a person is published, include publication status, policy/review date, expiry, public contact scope, and correction route where the profile requires it. Never publish home contact data, private schedules, HR identifiers, or protected characteristics.

Statistics must be genuinely aggregated, period-scoped, sourced, and subject to small-cell suppression. Transportation exposes public routes/stops/service areas and general schedules only. Facilities expose public descriptions/accessibility/hours, not access control, alarms, cameras, keys, or vulnerabilities. Meal allergens and dietary labels are source claims with update time and a disclaimer; they are not medical safety guarantees.

Agent and upload tooling must scan for privacy risks before candidate generation, quarantine prohibited material, redact reports, avoid retaining raw private data, and require human publication approval. The protocol is not legal advice and does not itself guarantee FERPA, GDPR, UK GDPR, COPPA, employment, or local-law compliance.
