# Pilot publisher checklist

- serve the exact well-known path over HTTPS;
- publish the Ecme or minimal fixture without modifying its fictional identifiers;
- support GET and HEAD with the documented content type, cache, and CORS behavior;
- record the served commit/archive checksum and implementation version;
- provide a correction contact and rollback plan;
- do not include student records, private staff data, credentials, internal endpoints, or live
  enrollment data;
- send the consumer only the public fixture URL and the redacted evidence bundle.

The publisher owns its origin and facts. A pilot is interoperability evidence, not certification or
paper&slate endorsement.
