# Pilot consumer checklist

- identify the exact origin, final URL, profile, and implementation version;
- enforce HTTPS and revalidate every redirect against SSRF/private-network policy;
- bound response bytes, redirects, graph depth, and cache age;
- reject duplicate JSON keys, invalid content type, unknown required fields, and broken references;
- distinguish structural validity, semantic validity, privacy policy, integrity, and authority;
- record only redacted status/findings and the fixture/report digests;
- do not infer school quality, legal compliance, factual truth, or publisher endorsement from a pass.

The consumer must attach the same fixture digest and its observed behavior to the feedback record.
